How to use on IOS? 

1. Open Safari on your iPhone and navigate to https://sleepingywn.github.io/tennis-checklist/.

2. Tap the Share button.

3. Scroll down the share sheet and tap Add to Home Screen.

4. Name it Tennis Checklist (or CourtReady) and tap Add in the top right corner.

5. Verification: Return to your iPhone Home Screen and tap the new app icon. It will open directly into your checklist, and checking items will automatically save to your phone's local storage.
